About this profile

The Rongo mā Tāne (peace, peaceful actions and productivity) segment of Te Ara Whakamana builds an individual profile that helps ākonga and their whānau share their sensory experiences. It gives kaiako and kaiāwhina insight into how sensory experiences connect with wellbeing and behaviour. Read it alongside the learner's Mana Plan.

We are all unique learners on a spectrum of difference. Adapting teaching to meet each learner's needs and strengths should be the norm, not the exception.

Seeks and avoids. Hyposensitive learners tend to seek out stimulation, or may not notice it. Hypersensitive learners tend to avoid it, because it feels like too much. Most of us sit somewhere in between, and it changes with the setting and the day.

The whakataukī. He toka tū moana, he akinga nā ngā tai. Ryan Meachen (Ngāti Te Wehi, Ngāti Huia) describes it as being the author of your own story: standing firm in who you are and living in line with your mauri.

Not a diagnostic tool. This profile shows strengths, needs and practical strategies. It does not sort learners into diagnostic categories. Where a formal assessment would help, this profile can support that pathway.
